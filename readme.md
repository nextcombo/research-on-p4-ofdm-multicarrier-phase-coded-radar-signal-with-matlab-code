# Research on P4-OFDM Multicarrier Phase Coded Radar Signal [with MATLAB code]

\n  
\tLow-Probability Detection Radar generally adopts broadband signals as the emission waveforms. Traditional broadband signals mainly include linear frequency modulation (LFM) signals and nonlinear frequency modulation (NLFM) signals, but both are susceptible to interception due to their low waveform complexity and concentrated signal energy in time-frequency distribution. Multi-carrier phase-coded (MCPC) signals combine phase coding with orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) technology, offering advantages such as low detection probability, anti-interference capability, digitalization, flexible waveforms, and high spectral efficiency. In recent years, these have become a hot topic in radar waveform research. This paper primarily explores the following research work around the key characteristics of MCPC signals:  
\t1. The structure of MCPC signals and radar systems  
\t2. Performance of P4 codes  
\t3. Analysis of P4-OFDM radar signals, including their time-domain waveforms, autocorrelation functions, and fuzzy functions, to discuss their performance.
